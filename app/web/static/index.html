On air
Turn on as many sports as you like. The panel cycles them one league at a time in the order shown — every live NFL game, then every live MLB game — and you get alerts from all of them.
Show only my favourite teams
Per sport, and it covers live games and the upcoming schedule alike — so the whole NFL slate plus only your two baseball teams is a valid setup. Pick favourites under Setup → Favourite teams.
Upcoming games
Cycle each sport's upcoming games — today's slate for baseball, this week's schedule for football — instead of an idle screen. College football follows whatever categories you picked on the Panel tab, so “Top 25” means twenty-five games, not ninety-nine.
Keep showing them while a game is live
Per sport, because it isn't one question. With two baseball games on you might want those two plus the NFL's week ahead — and specifically not the rest of today's baseball. Live games still come first within their own sport.
